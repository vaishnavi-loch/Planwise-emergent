## Which Option Is Right For You?

**Planwise Partner**
 Suitable if you'd like ongoing access to guidance as new questions or decisions come up over time, without a fixed structure to it.

**Family Navigator**
 Suitable if you're supporting a family member and want the whole family coordinated around one shared plan, with regular check-ins and help navigating paperwork like My Aged Care.

**Ageing Well Comprehensive Package**
 Suitable if you want a full plan done in one go, covering your situation across all 4 Pillars, with a written report and short-term follow-up support to help you act on it.

If you're unsure which option is right for you, we'll discuss this during your free discovery call.

## Transparent Pricing, Independent Advice

Planwise operates on a fee-for-service model. That means we are paid by you, not by aged care providers or referral partners.

This allows us to provide independent guidance based on your circumstances, goals and preferences, without being influenced by commissions or referral incentives.

Our pricing is simple and transparent, so you can focus on choosing the support that's right for you.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How much does **Planwise** cost?**

We offer three ways to work with us: Planwise Partner for $49 per month (minimum 3 months), Family Navigator for $79 per month (minimum 6 months) for up to 3 family members, or the Ageing Well Comprehensive Package for $495 one-off. Every plan starts with a free 15-minute discovery call.

**Which membership is right for me?**

That depends on the level of support you need. If you have a specific question or decision, on-demand support may be suitable. If you'd like ongoing guidance, a membership may be the better option.

**Is there a free option to start?**

Yes. We offer a free 15-minute discovery call so you can discuss your situation, ask questions and decide whether Planwise is right for you.

**Can I cancel my membership?**

Please refer to your membership agreement or speak with the Planwise team for information about cancellation terms and ongoing support options.* *

**Do you receive commissions from providers?**

No. Planwise does not receive commissions or referral incentives from aged care providers. Our recommendations are based solely on your circumstances and goals.
