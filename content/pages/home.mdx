## The 4 Pillars Of Ageing Well

Every decision about later life is interconnected. Our planning framework considers every area that contributes to living well now and into the future.

### Purpose

Maintaining purpose, social connection and meaningful activities is an important and often overlooked part of ageing well. We help you identify opportunities that support confidence, wellbeing and an active lifestyle.

### Financial And Legal

Planning ahead includes understanding the financial and legal decisions that may arise over time. We help you identify what to consider and connect you with trusted professionals where appropriate.

### Mind And Body

Your physical and emotional wellbeing both play a role in ageing well. We help you explore services, supports and healthy lifestyle options that align with your needs and goals.

### Where And How You Live

Whether you want to remain at home, explore retirement living or consider residential aged care, we help you understand your options and plan for the future.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is Planwise and how can it help me?

Planwise is a premium coordination and planning service for ageing well. We assess your needs and wants, make recommendations, help you understand your options, and connect with suitable services when needed.

### Who is Planwise for?

Planwise supports older Australians planning ahead, families supporting loved ones, and anyone looking for independent guidance about later life decisions and ageing well.

### What does Planwise cost?

We offer three ways to work with us: Planwise Partner at $49 per month (minimum 3 months), Family Navigator at $79 per month (minimum 6 months) for up to 3 family members, or the Ageing Well Comprehensive Package at $495 one-off. A free 15-minute discovery call is available to discuss your needs and the right option for you.

### Is Planwise independent from providers?

Yes. Planwise does not receive commissions from aged care providers or any referral partners from our quality referral network. This allows us to provide guidance based on your circumstances and preferences.

### Do I need to be receiving aged care services to use Planwise?

No. You can work with Planwise before you need aged care services. Many people start planning earlier to understand their options and prepare for the future.
