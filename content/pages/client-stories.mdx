## Client Stories

### Helping a Family Make Aged Care Decisions

**The situation:** Barbara and her husband David needed to consider accommodation and care options as David's health needs increased. Their son reached out to Planwise on the family's behalf.

**What we did:** We helped the family understand aged care accommodation options, connected them with a financial adviser to work through selling the family home,, and assessed suitable care providers for David.

**The outcome:** Barbara later became an ongoing Planwise client, so she could keep working through David's care while looking after her own wellbeing too.

### Downsizing and Relocating with Confidence

**The situation:** Yvonne decided it was time to downsize and move closer to public transport to feel more connected and independent, but wanted support with the practical side of the move.

**What we did:** We helped coordinate the sale of her car and furniture, clarified the financial aspects of the move, connected her with professionals who could review her walking capacity and general fitness, and helped her consider how to stay connected to her GP, services, and local activities in her new area.

**The outcome:** Yvonne moved with a clear plan in place, keeping her mobility and social connections intact. She approached the next chapter with energy and confidence.

### Managing a Property and Asset Sale

**The situation:** After relocating from regional Victoria to the city, Trevor needed to sell his unit and car with no prior experience doing either.

**What we did:** We connected Trevor with an Advisor who researched local real estate agents, compiled appraisals into a clear report, arranged pre-sale preparation, and organised the sale of his car through a broker.

**The outcome:** Trevor reviewed the report, chose his preferred agent, with both the unit and car sold within six weeks.

### Finding New Purpose in Retirement

**The situation:** After retiring, Theresa found herself without a clear plan for how to spend her time and unsure what came next.

**What we did:** We worked with Theresa on the Purpose pillar, offering options across part-time work, volunteering, and recreational programs, along with guidance on staying active and reviewing her finances to support her new routine.

**The outcome:** Theresa began volunteering with a local community group, started exploring contract and consulting work for the challenge and flexibility it offered, and joined a gym suited to her fitness level, building a routine that worked for her.

### Supporting a Parent While Managing Work and Family

**The situation:** Anthony was balancing full-time work and family life while supporting his 92-year-old mother, Sylvie, with day-to-day tasks and transport.

**What we did:** We referred Sylvie to trusted carer services, connected her with a physiotherapist and occupational therapist, and referred Anthony to Carer Gateway for his own support. Ongoing check-ins ensured the plan continued to suit Sylvie's needs.

**The outcome:** Sylvie received the support she needed to maintain her routine and independence, and Anthony's day-to-day load eased as a result.

### Coordinating Care Through a Health Decline

**The situation:** When Levi's health declined following a serious diagnosis, his daughter Anastasia needed to make a number of care decisions in a short period of time.

**What we did:** We identified the care packages Levi was eligible for, guided Anastasia through the assessment process, arranged home equipment and care providers, and referred the family to palliative care support.

**The outcome:** Levi was able to remain at home with the right level of care in place, and Anastasia had a clear plan to work from.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Are these real client stories?**

Yes. These stories are based on real experiences of people and families who have worked with Planwise. We only share client stories with permission or after removing names and identifying details to protect privacy.

**Can **Planwise** help if I'm **planning ahead**?**

Yes. Many people work with us before they need aged care services or additional support. Planning ahead can help you understand your options and prepare for future decisions.

**Can you help if I'm supporting an ageing parent?**

Yes. We regularly work with adult children, partners and other family members to help them understand options and make informed decisions together.

**What kinds of situations does **Planwise** help with?**

We support people with a wide range of later life decisions, including planning ahead, finding purpose and direction after retirement, home support, aged care, housing, staying connected to community, and accessing trusted services.

**Do I have to research everything myself?**

No. There's a huge amount of information available about ageing, aged care, and later life planning, and working out what's actually relevant to your situation takes time. We've already done that groundwork, so instead of spending hours piecing it together yourself, you get guidance and options that are already relevant to you.

**Can you help if I don't know where to start?**

Yes. Many people contact us because they're unsure about their options. We'll take the time to understand your situation and help you identify the next steps that are right for you. We are available to answer your questions whenever they arise.

**How do I get started?**

Start with a free discovery call. We'll discuss your situation, answer your questions and help you decide which level of support is right for you.
