# Purpose: Staying Connected, Engaged and Fulfilled

Retirement changes more than your schedule. It can change how connected you feel, how confident you are, and what your day is actually built around. We help you discover what gives you meaning and joy, so you can stay engaged in life on your own terms.

## Why Purpose Matters as We Age

A sense of purpose is closely linked to health and wellbeing in later life. People who stay socially connected and engaged in meaningful activity tend to report better mental health, more confidence and greater life satisfaction as they age.

For many people, identity has been tied to paid work for decades. When that ends, so does a routine, a social circle and a sense of contribution, all at once. Active ageing isn't about staying busy for the sake of it. It's about rebuilding a routine and a sense of self around what matters to you now.

## Common Challenges

Retirement and later life bring changes that are rarely talked about until they're already happening:

### Loss of Role

Without a job title or daily responsibilities, it can be hard to answer "what do I do now?"

### Less Social Contact

Workplaces, school runs and everyday routines create social contact almost by accident. Once they're gone, that contact has to be built deliberately.

### Lower Confidence

Fewer chances to contribute or be needed can chip away at self-esteem over time.

### Isolation

Loneliness in older adults is common, and it often builds gradually rather than arriving all at once.

None of these are signs that something has gone wrong. They're a normal part of a major life transition, and they respond well to the right support.

## How Planwise Helps You Find Purpose

We help you identify activities and pursuits that give meaning and joy. That means finding the right mix of social connection, meaningful contribution and ongoing challenge for your situation.

### Reconnecting Socially

Staying connected in older age often means rebuilding a social life that used to happen automatically through work or family routines. We help you find social groups for seniors and community activities that are enjoyable in themselves, not just a box to tick, whether that's a local club, a regular catch-up group or a shared interest activity.

### Meaningful Activities and Volunteering

Volunteering for over 60s is one of the most effective ways to combine social connection with a genuine sense of contribution. We help you find opportunities where you're both giving something and getting something back, whether that's through structured volunteering, community involvement or informal ways of helping others.

### Sharing Skills and Staying Challenged

Many people want more than social contact. They want to keep learning, keep contributing and stay mentally stimulated. We help you find ways to share your skills and experience, whether through mentoring, part-time work in retirement, or lifelong learning options like University of the Third Age (U3A) or a local Men's Shed. We also help you work through whatever's been holding you back from getting started.

## Purpose in Practice

### Theresa's Search for New Purpose in Retirement

**The situation:** After retiring, Theresa found herself without a clear plan for how to spend her time and unsure what came next.

**What we did:** We worked with Theresa on the Purpose pillar, offering options across part-time work, volunteering, and recreational programs, along with guidance on staying active and reviewing her finances to support her new routine.

**The outcome:** Theresa began volunteering with a local community group, started exploring contract and consulting work for the challenge and flexibility it offered, and joined a gym suited to her fitness level, building a routine that worked for her.
