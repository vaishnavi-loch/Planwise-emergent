## Why Financial & Legal Planning Matters as We Age

Financial and legal planning is easiest to do while there's no urgency behind it. Waiting until a health crisis forces the decision often means less choice, more stress, and decisions made under pressure rather than with a clear head.

In Victoria, if these documents aren't in place before someone loses capacity, family members can't simply step in. They need to apply to VCAT (the Victorian Civil and Administrative Tribunal) for guardianship or administration, a process that takes time and where the tribunal, not the family, decides who's appointed and on what terms. Planning ahead avoids that.

## Common Challenges

Financial and legal planning for later life brings up questions many people put off until they can't be avoided:

### Not Knowing Where to Start

Superannuation, wills, powers of attorney and aged care costs can all feel like separate, overwhelming problems.

### Uncertainty About Capacity

Families often aren't sure what happens if a loved one is no longer able to make their own financial decisions.

### Fear of Getting It Wrong

Without independent guidance, it's hard to know which professional to trust or what questions to even ask.

### Vulnerability to Financial Abuse

Older Australians can be targets of financial exploitation, sometimes from people close to them.

None of these are signs that something has gone wrong. They're common, and they respond well to early planning and the right support.

## How Planwise Helps You Plan with Confidence

We help you work through the financial and legal side of later life step by step, connecting you with the right professional for each decision.

### Financial Planning for Later Life

Retirement brings financial questions that don't come up earlier in life, like how much superannuation is enough, how to budget on a reduced income, and how to plan for aged care costs if they arise. We help you identify what to think about and where a qualified financial adviser's input would help.

### Protecting Yourself and Your Decisions

An enduring power of attorney lets you choose, in advance, who makes financial or personal decisions on your behalf if you're ever unable to. We help you understand the difference between this and guardianship through VCAT, when a capacity assessment may be needed, and how to protect against elder financial abuse.

### Estate and End-of-Life Planning

Wills, advance care directives and funeral planning are easier to think through before they're urgently needed. We help you understand what each document covers and connect you with the right professional to put them in place.
