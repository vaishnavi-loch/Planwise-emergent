# About Planwise

Planwise was founded to help older Australians and their families make informed decisions about later life with independent guidance and practical support.

Our team brings together experience in nursing, psychology and care coordination to help you understand your options, plan, and make decisions that reflect your goals and circumstances.

## Our Story

The idea for Planwise came from years of supporting older Australians and their families. We saw firsthand how challenging it could be to make informed decisions when support was spread across different services and providers.

Planning for later life often involves decisions about how you want to spend your time, your health, your housing, your finances and the support you may need. Many people aren't sure where to begin or who they can turn to for independent guidance.

We believed there should be a more connected approach. Rather than focusing on one service or one stage of ageing, we wanted to help people see the bigger picture and make informed decisions that reflect their goals and circumstances.

That belief led to the creation of Planwise, an independent premium coordination and planning service that brings together the different aspects of ageing well into one personalised plan.

## Our Approach

We believe ageing well is about more than addressing one need at a time. Health, purpose, finances and the way you live are all connected, and decisions in one area often influence another.

That's why Planwise is built around the Four Pillars of Ageing Well. Built on clinical experience and healthy ageing research, this framework supports informed planning and better outcomes for later life.

### Purpose

A sense of purpose contributes to wellbeing at every stage of life. We help you stay connected to the people, activities and opportunities that matter most to you.

### Financial & Legal

Planning ahead can make future decisions easier. While we don't provide financial or legal advice, we help you identify where professional guidance may form part of your broader plan.

### Mind & Body

Physical and mental wellbeing play an important role in ageing well. We help you consider the support, services and lifestyle choices that align with your needs and goals.

### Where & How You Live

Your living environment influences your independence and quality of life. We help you explore housing, home support and care options that suit your circumstances.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Who runs **Planwise**?**

Planwise is led by a Registered Nurse and a Registered Psychologist with experience in care coordination, healthy ageing and supporting older Australians through later life planning.

**Is **Planwise** independent from providers?**

Yes. We do not receive commissions or referral incentives from service providers. Our recommendations are based on your circumstances, goals and preferences.

**How do you choose the providers you recommend?**

We recommend providers after understanding your needs, goals and preferences. Recommendations are based on suitability and are never influenced by commissions or commercial arrangements. We have vetted the services and providers that make up our referral network. We also welcome feedback from our clients about their experience with services we have recommended, so we can maintain the high quality our clients expect.
