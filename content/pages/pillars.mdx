# The 4 Pillars Of Ageing Well

Ageing well starts with understanding what matters most to you. It means considering the different areas of your life that shape your future.

At Planwise, we use the 4 Pillars of Ageing Well to help you take a whole-person approach to planning for later life. Together, they support your wellbeing, independence and quality of life.

## What Ageing Well Really Means

There's more to ageing well than good health. It's about feeling connected, having your finances and legal affairs in order, living somewhere that suits you, and having a sense of purpose in your day-to-day life.

We built our approach around four pillars, Purpose, Legal and Financial, Mind and Body, and Where and How You Live, because each one plays a part in a life that feels full and well-supported.

## Pillar 1: Purpose

Having a sense of purpose helps you stay connected, engaged and involved in the activities that matter to you. Whether it's spending time with family, volunteering or pursuing new interests, this pillar focuses on maintaining meaning and connection throughout later life.

**How we help**

We help you identify opportunities, services and activities that support social connection, community participation and a meaningful later life.

## Pillar 2: Financial & Legal

Planning ahead can make future decisions easier. This pillar considers the legal and financial planning that supports later life and helps you identify where professional advice may be beneficial.

## Pillar 3: Mind & Body

Physical and mental wellbeing both play an important role in healthy ageing. This pillar considers your health, lifestyle, emotional wellbeing and the support that helps you stay active and independent.

**How we help**

## Pillar 4: Where & How You Live

Where you live plays an important role in your independence and quality of life. Whether you're planning to stay at home, explore retirement living or consider residential aged care, this pillar helps you understand your options.

**How we help**

## How The Pillars Work Together

The four pillars are designed to work together because decisions in one area often influence another.

For example, a change in your living arrangements may affect your social connections, finances and support needs. Looking at the whole picture helps you make informed decisions that reflect your goals and circumstances.

Whether you're planning ahead or responding to a change in your situation, Planwise helps you understand how each pillar fits into your personalised plan.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What are the 4 Pillars of Ageing Well?**

The 4 Pillars of Ageing Well are Purpose, Legal & Financial, Mind & Body, and Where & How You Live. Together, they provide a framework for planning later life and considering the different factors that contribute to wellbeing and independence.

**What does ageing well actually mean?**

Ageing well means maintaining your wellbeing, independence and quality of life as your needs and circumstances change. It involves more than health alone and includes your relationships, living arrangements, financial planning and sense of purpose.

**Do I have to use all four pillars?**

Not necessarily. Some people may focus on one area, while others benefit from considering all four. The framework is flexible and designed to reflect your individual circumstances and priorities.
