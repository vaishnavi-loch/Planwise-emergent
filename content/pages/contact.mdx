## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I contact **Planwise**?**

You can contact us by phone, email or by completing the enquiry form on this page. You can also book a free 15-minute discovery call online.

**How do I book a free discovery call?**

Simply use the booking button on this page to choose a time that suits you. If you prefer, you can also contact us by phone or email to arrange a call.

**Do you offer a free consultation?**

Yes. We offer a free 15-minute discovery call to help you discuss your situation, ask questions and learn how Planwise can support you.

**Can family members contact **Planwise**?**

Yes. We regularly support adult children, spouses, carers and other family members who are helping an older loved one plan for later life.

**Do you help people outside Melbourne?**

Yes. Depending on your location and needs, we can provide support by phone or video. Contact us to discuss the most suitable option.
